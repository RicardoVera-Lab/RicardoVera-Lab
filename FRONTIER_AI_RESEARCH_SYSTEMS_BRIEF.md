# Ricardo Vera / CIEC LAB — Frontier AI Research Systems Capability Brief

## AI is not the deliverable. Decision-grade evidence is.

I design and operate **AI-assisted research systems for consequential problems where model output alone is not enough**.

The operating question is not “can an AI produce an answer?”

It is:

> **Can a human–AI research system produce a result whose evidence, uncertainty, failure modes, dissent and stopping conditions remain visible enough to support a real decision?**

CIEC LAB is the independent laboratory I built to test that idea in public.

---

## What I actually built

CIEC LAB uses frontier AI as **research infrastructure**, not as a single conversational assistant.

At a high level, the system is designed to:

- decompose ambiguous research objectives into bounded missions;
- separate exploration from validation and final decision;
- preserve competing hypotheses and adversarial dissent;
- distinguish claims, evidence, inference and unresolved uncertainty;
- route different research tasks to different capabilities;
- require reproducibility or verification paths where the object permits it;
- freeze success, failure and stop conditions before results are interpreted;
- retain negative results rather than forcing a positive narrative;
- keep public evidence auditable while proprietary orchestration remains private.

The internal orchestration, prompts, routing rules and decision thresholds are proprietary.

The public artifacts expose the claims, evidence boundaries and verification surfaces needed to evaluate the work.

## Three public proof objects

### 1. DISCOVER — H6

**Universal Quadratic Image Theorem**

A computer-assisted mathematical result establishing the frozen lower bound:

**h(6) ≥ 25**

The public package includes proof architecture, exact certificate material, witness data, a clean-room verifier and automated verification.

**What it demonstrates:** an AI-assisted research process can be pushed beyond ideation into a formal positive result structured for adversarial attack and reproducible checking.

[Open H6](https://github.com/RicardoVera-Lab/h6-universal-quadratic-image-theorem)

---

### 2. CHALLENGE — CS06

**Hypnosis Mechanism Identification Under Uncertainty**

A strong initial mechanism was decomposed into rival explanations, stress-tested against measurement limitations and closed without promoting a preferred story beyond the evidence.

**What it demonstrates:** the system can use AI to challenge an attractive hypothesis, identify the actual evidence bottleneck and preserve a bounded unresolved result when the data do not justify a stronger claim.

[Open CS06](https://github.com/RicardoVera-Lab/cs06-hypnosis-mechanism-identification-case-study)

---

### 3. VERIFY — CS05

**Post-Drive Topological Polarization Overshoot**

A published physical anomaly was converted into a preregistered discrimination test before access to the desired raw data. The analysis logic was frozen and validated against adversarial synthetic cases. When the required real evidence could not be obtained at the necessary standard, the investigation stopped rather than substituting weaker evidence.

**What it demonstrates:** evidence boundaries, preregistration and stopping rules can be enforced even when the more exciting outcome would be to keep going.

[Open CS05](https://github.com/RicardoVera-Lab/cs05-topological-overshoot-case-study)

## What I am not claiming

Credibility requires explicit boundaries.

I am **not** claiming that:

- CIEC LAB has externally validated a new general theory of AI orchestration;
- these public cases substitute for production deployment experience inside a frontier-model company;
- the laboratory architecture is itself a benchmark result;
- I should be evaluated as a pure frontier-model training researcher on the basis of these artifacts alone.

The claim is narrower:

> **I have built and operated a working research environment that treats frontier AI as a governed system of inquiry — with explicit evidence boundaries, adversarial challenge, reproducibility surfaces and permission to return NO-GO or UNRESOLVED.**

That is the capability I want to put in contact with teams working on the next generation of AI-assisted research and decision systems.
