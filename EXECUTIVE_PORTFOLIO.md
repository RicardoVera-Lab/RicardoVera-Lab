# CIEC LAB — Executive Portfolio

## High-consequence research for decisions that cannot afford false confidence

**CIEC LAB attacks uncertainty before it becomes capital loss.**

The laboratory is designed for technical and scientific decisions where the answer is not clean, the evidence is incomplete, and a confident error can become embedded in R&D spend, product architecture, investment, acquisition, model deployment or public commitment.

The operating objective is simple:

> **turn an uncertain technical claim into a decision that can survive scrutiny.**

---

## The capability

CIEC LAB combines:

- adversarial research;
- mathematical and computational reasoning;
- hypothesis discrimination;
- evidence and provenance control;
- exact or reproducible verification where possible;
- explicit claim boundaries;
- stopping rules;
- decision-oriented synthesis.

The system is deliberately capable of returning **positive results, negative results, bounded uncertainty or a hard stop**.

A research system that always returns the exciting answer is not a high-reliability research system.

## Public evidence

### DISCOVER — H6

**Universal Quadratic Image Theorem**

A computer-assisted mathematical result establishing the frozen lower bound:

**h(6) ≥ 25**

Public evidence includes the preprint, proof architecture, exact certificate material, a clean-room verifier, witness data, adversarial attacks and automated verification.

**Executive meaning:** the laboratory can enter a difficult unresolved technical object and exit with a positive result that is structured for attack and verification.

[Open H6](https://github.com/RicardoVera-Lab/h6-universal-quadratic-image-theorem)

---

### CHALLENGE — CS06

**Hypnosis Mechanism Identification Under Uncertainty**

A strong initial hypothesis was decomposed into rival mechanisms, stress-tested against measurement limitations and closed without promoting a preferred story beyond the evidence.

**Executive meaning:** the laboratory can protect a decision from a seductive technical narrative, identify the real evidence bottleneck and determine when additional internal analysis no longer changes the decision.

[Open CS06](https://github.com/RicardoVera-Lab/cs06-hypnosis-mechanism-identification-case-study)

---

### VERIFY — CS05

**Post-Drive Topological Polarization Overshoot**

A published physical anomaly was converted into a preregistered discrimination test. The analysis was frozen before access to the desired real data and validated against adversarial synthetic cases. When the required evidence could not be obtained, the laboratory stopped instead of replacing it with weaker evidence.

**Executive meaning:** the laboratory can enforce evidence boundaries under pressure and prevent a weak substitute from becoming a confident technical claim.

[Open CS05](https://github.com/RicardoVera-Lab/cs05-topological-overshoot-case-study)

## What this portfolio is designed to prove

Not that CIEC LAB always finds a positive answer.

Something more valuable:

> **the laboratory can distinguish what is proved, what is plausible, what is unresolved, what would falsify the current story, and when it is rational to stop.**

That behavior transfers to:

- frontier R&D;
- technical due diligence;
- model and algorithm validation;
- scientific intelligence;
- experimental design;
- technology assessment;
- high-uncertainty strategy;
- evidence architecture.

---

## What high-consequence clients actually buy

Not more analysis.

They buy a lower probability of being confidently wrong when the mistake is expensive.

A strong engagement should make the following explicit:

- the strongest defensible claim;
- the strongest rival explanation;
- the evidence that matters;
- the evidence that is missing;
- the failure modes;
- the minimum next test worth funding;
- the boundary between **continue, redesign, kill or commit**.

---

## Engagement trigger

CIEC LAB is most valuable before a decision becomes costly to reverse.

Bring one:

- claim;
- model;
- technical thesis;
- R&D hypothesis;
- vendor or acquisition assumption;
- scientific interpretation;
- high-consequence decision object.

**One claim is enough to start.**

---

## Public evidence. Private machinery.

The portfolio is intentionally asymmetric.

The claims, evidence, limitations and public verification paths are exposed.

The internal architecture, orchestration, prompts, routing, thresholds and decision machinery are not.

> **Public evidence. Private machinery.**
